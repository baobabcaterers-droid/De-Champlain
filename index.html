02 · Why Explore?

Reasons for Exploring

To enrich the empire by claiming new land and expanding its trade in fur with the Natives of Turtle Island. New territories could increase its reach and give traders access to more sources of valuable animal pelts. By collecting and selling fur, the empire hoped to bring in wealth and strengthen its control.

Arrival

Sailing from France under the French crown, he reached the Atlantic coast of present-day Nova Scotia and the Bay of Fundy region.

Port Royal

He co-founded Port Royal in Nova Scotia, one of the first European settlements in the region.

Mapping the coast

He mapped a section of the East Coast, charting harbours and rivers for future French trade.

A lake with his name

He was the first European to see the lake that would later bear his name, on the same day as its discovery.

04 · Impressions

What Did He Think?

A land of massive economic, strategic and geographic potential — rather than one unified country.

Champlain tried to integrate the people he met into European ways of life and to convert them to Catholic Christianity. He met many First Nations on his travels, and their knowledge of the land, rivers and trade routes shaped nearly everything he was able to do.

05 · Relationships

First Nations & Champlain

Champlain used diplomacy and friendly words to win trust, because strong alliances meant secure access to furs. He built partnerships with several nations, but those partnerships served French goals, and his efforts to change Indigenous beliefs and ways of life had lasting consequences for the communities involved.
